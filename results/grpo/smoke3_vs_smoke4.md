# Smoke run 3 vs smoke run 4

Both: 20 GRPO steps from base moshiko with a fresh LoRA r64, same seed and rollout starts, 4 train starts x 16
games of 12 user turns per step, scorer v3 (only a bare fruit is valid). Configs: `configs/grpo_smoke3.yaml`,
`configs/grpo_smoke4.yaml`. Differences in smoke 4:

| | smoke 3 | smoke 4 |
|---|---|---|
| policy gradient | text + audio tokens | **text tokens only** (audio_weight 0) |
| KL to base (k3) | beta 0.04 on text and audio | **text 0.04, audio 0.2** |
| overlap penalty | -0.8, 0.2 s grace | **-1.0, 0.1 s grace** |
| valid fruit with counted overlap | capped at +0.2 | **capped at 0.0** |
| validation | steps 0, 20 | steps 0, 10, 20 |

## Training curves

- Train bare-valid at step 20: smoke 3 70.9%, smoke 4 55.2%; peak smoke 3 74.9%, smoke 4 55.2%.
- Silent phase: smoke 3 peaked at 73% silent (step 8), smoke 4 at 87% (step 8); smoke 4 left it about 2-3 steps later.
- Time per step: smoke 3 62 s, smoke 4 53 s (mean); 45 GB peak per GPU.
- Plots: `results/plots/grpo_compare_grpo_smoke3_vs_grpo_smoke4.png` (+ `_val.png`), `results/plots/grpo_smoke4/`.

## Reading

- Smoke 4's audio stays much closer to base Moshi (audio KL 0.006 vs 0.037 at step 20) and Whisper hears more of
  its fruits (93.7% vs 89.3%, per-turn class agreement 78% vs 66%).
- The stricter overlap rule did not lower the overlap that counts (16.0% vs 16.4% at the 0.1 s grace, 10.1% vs
  9.8% at 0.2 s), but fewer valid fruits start before the user finishes (29.7% vs 38.0%).
- It costs bare-valid answers at step 20 (42.9% vs 52.7%), mostly through a longer silent phase and more late
  repeats (turns 7-12: 31.8% vs 28.2%). Smoke 4 was still rising at step 20 (train valid 55% at step 20, smoke 3 71%).
- Samples: releases `grpo-smoke3` and `grpo-smoke4` (best / median / worst step-20 training games).
